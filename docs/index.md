This site publishes UCA TF14's releases of the CIM Enterprise package (formerly IEC61968). It includes the release that corresponds to the current International Standard as well as the interim releases of work in progress toward the next edition of IEC 61968-11.

For the current International Standard, the site provides the UML model of IEC 61968-11 Ed 2.0 ([Ed 2.0 UML release]) and comparison reports between published editions. Each interim release is listed with its release notes and a [cim-compare](https://cim-compare.ucaiug.io/) report of what changed in the UML. The interim models themselves (Sparx EA project files) are available only to TF14 participants, through the UCA TF14 site, until the final stable release is made public, so active participation is encouraged.

Edition 3 of IEC 61968-11 is in preparation; its UML package, CIM13v13b, is available on the [CIMug site](https://cimug.org/cimdocs/standards-artifacts/).

Comparison reports were generated with cim-compare [x.y.z].
